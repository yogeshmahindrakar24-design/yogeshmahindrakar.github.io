How I Handle a Bug

Jira defect lifecycle

Every bug I raise follows the same six steps, start to finish.

1

Raise

Log the bug with clear steps, screenshots, severity, and expected vs. actual behaviour.

2

Explain

Walk the developer through it so they understand exactly what's wrong and how to reproduce it.

3

Track

Check status regularly (Open, In Progress, Resolved) so nothing gets forgotten.

4

Retest

Once marked fixed, I test it again to confirm it's actually resolved.

5

Regress

Check the surrounding area to make sure the fix didn't break something else.

6

Close

Mark it closed in Jira and fold it into the release's test summary.
